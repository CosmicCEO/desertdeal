# Desert Deal — pitch (text)

**DESERT DEAL**  
*A worn pack of cards. One player. The desert table.*

Solo **Klondike** solitaire in the kraft paper look of Desert Ranger and Desert Bandit. A dog-eared 52-card deck with coffee stains, dog-ears, and sun-bleach. Drag and fling cards with real motion. Save mid-deal. Keep your high score and best time.

- Solo Klondike (Draw 3)
- Worn paper deck — defects layered on cream stock
- Physics drag / fling / flip (CSS/JS)
- Save + plays / high score / best time (local)
- Sibling to Desert Ranger & Desert Bandit

Static web app. No download. No account. © 2026 Jerod Price / CosmicCEO.
