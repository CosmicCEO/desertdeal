# Desert Deal — pitch (text)

**DESERT DEAL**  
*A dog-eared pack on a worn wooden picnic table.*

Solo **Klondike** solitaire in the desert family of Desert Ranger and Desert Bandit. Weathered planks with grain, knots, silver-grey sun bleach, dark gaps, and flush bolt heads. A worn 52-card deck with coffee stains, dog-ears, and sun-bleach. Drag and fling cards with real motion. Save mid-deal. Keep your high score and best time. (Optional ants on the boards.)
